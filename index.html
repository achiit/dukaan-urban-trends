The shop

Fifteen years, one road, same promise

AK
Arshil KhanOwner, Urban Threads

When my uncle and I put up our first rack near Dakhin Darwaza in 2011, we had maybe forty pieces and one rule: never sell something we wouldn't wear ourselves. That rule is still taped to the wall behind the counter.

Basti has changed a lot since then. The boys who came in for a ₹250 tee in school now bring their younger brothers. Fashion moved to oversized fits and wide-leg denim, so we moved with it, but we kept the prices honest.

Come in, touch the cotton, try the fit. If you can't come, send me a message and I'll send photos myself.

  • Cotton you can feelSoft, breathable fabric picked for Basti summers.
  • Prices said out loud₹250 tee, ₹500 shirt, ₹800 jeans. No haggling theatre.
  • Current cutsDown-shoulder tees, wide-leg denim, relaxed shirts.
